The Seventh Cycle, in the Open

Every eight years the state tells the San Diego region how many new homes to plan for, and SANDAG splits the number among 19 cities. This project rebuilt the last split from public documents, tested it against the rules coming in 2028, and publishes everything — the data, the code, and every step between — so that any city can recompute its own number. Start with the walkthrough.
