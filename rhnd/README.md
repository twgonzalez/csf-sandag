# SANDAG 7th-cycle RHND — what was run and what it found

Regenerated by the `rhnd/` module (`.venv/bin/python -m rhnd.run` for the
determination and validation, `.venv/bin/python -m rhnd.jurisdiction` for the
jurisdiction table and gap report). Every number traces to a file in
`data/raw/` through `rhnd/` (see `data/manifest.yaml`); tests in
`tests/test_rhnd.py` and `tests/known_figures.yaml` hold the reproduction to
its tolerances.

## Method

HCD's 7th-cycle RHND methodology as published in its Mono County final
determination (Jan 23, 2026, corrected), validated by reproducing that letter
line-by-line from public data (`validation_mono.md`): every adjustment line
within 0–4 units except the PUMS overlap (a PUMA-geography limitation that
does not affect San Diego). The Humboldt determination (July 2025, HCD's own
letter) is the second anchor and reproduces exactly on lines 5a, 6, 7, 8, 9:
the two letters use the same ≥30% cost-burden and >1 person/room rates but
different formulas for lines 6–7 (Humboldt: units to dilute the region to the
US rate; Mono: rate difference × households), so both formulas are run as
scenarios.

## Central estimate

**~229,000 units** (228,584): Mono-letter method and vintages (2019–2023 ACS,
LODES 2022, PIT 2024, pre-revision DOF 2024-Baseline projections), projection
period Dec 31, 2028 → Jun 15, 2039 (housing elements due Jun 15, 2031).
The 6th-cycle determination was 171,685.

## Band: 179,000 – 229,000

The band comes from real alternatives, not invented uncertainty:

| driver | effect on total |
|---|---|
| Lines 6–7 formula: rate-difference (Mono letter, Jan 2026) vs dilution-to-US-rate (Humboldt letter, Jul 2025) | −43,000 under dilution |
| ACS/LODES/PIT vintage: 2019–23/2022/2024 vs 2020–24/2023/2025 | −4,700 newest |
| Projection-period end: Jun 15, 2039 vs Jan 31, 2039 (ABAG-schedule alternate) | −1,800 earlier |

All eight scenario totals are in `rhnd_7th_cycle_sandag.csv`. A further
residual not in the band: HCD's growth block comes from unpublished DOF
extrapolations; our public-file version reproduced Mono's within ~2%
(≈ a few thousand units at SANDAG scale, documented in `validation_mono.md`).

## The three largest lines (central scenario)

1. **Cost burden +136,160** (+94,263 under the Humboldt-letter formula) —
   43.4% of San Diego households pay ≥30% of income for housing vs 31.7%
   nationally. This line did not exist in SANDAG's 6th-cycle determination
   and is the single biggest reason the 7th-cycle number is larger.
2. **Household growth +54,405** — DOF-projected households at Jun 2039 minus
   projected occupied units at Dec 2028 (the only line that dominated the
   6th cycle, where it was ~95,000).
3. **Overcrowding +38,201** (less a −34,463 overlap credit for households
   both overcrowded and cost-burdened) — 6.69% of households over 1
   person/room vs 3.40% nationally.

Also notable: vacancy +26,725 (owner 0.72% vs 2% healthy, renter 3.98% vs 7%
healthy); homelessness +3,965 (PIT 2024 × household-formation rate); the
jobs/housing line is **0** — San Diego County is a net out-commuter in LODES
JT01 (−13,837) — and the feasibility line is 0 (1.19 projected jobs per unit
vs the 1.5 threshold).

## Files

- `rhnd_7th_cycle_sandag.csv` / `.md` — the determination, all scenarios,
  HCD Attachment-2 layout with the 6th-cycle column.
- `validation_mono.md` — Mono and Humboldt reproductions, line by line, with
  the documented irreducible differences.
- `jurisdiction_existing_need.csv` — what each of the 19 jurisdictions'
  residents generate under HCD's definitions (computed vs distributed columns
  flagged in the header; **not an allocation**).
- `jurisdiction_gap.md` — generated need vs what SANDAG's adopted 6th-cycle
  method (65% transit / 35% jobs) would allocate at the new regional total,
  sorted by gap.

## Rerun triggers

Parameterized on ACS vintage, LODES year, PIT year, DOF source, cost-burden
variant, and projection dates (`rhnd/run.py`). Rerun when: the 2021–2025 ACS
lands (Dec 2026, HCD's likely vintage), LODES 2024, the 2026 PIT, any new DOF
projection release, or a SANDAG draft 7th-cycle timeline that moves the due
date.
