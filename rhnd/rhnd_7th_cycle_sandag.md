# Draft 7th-cycle Regional Housing Need Determination - SANDAG

Regenerated by `rhnd/run.py sandag`. Method: HCD's 7th-cycle
methodology as published in the Mono County final determination
(2026-01-23) and validated line-by-line in validation_mono.md.
This is a forecast of HCD's 2028 determination, not an HCD
document.

**Central estimate: 228,584 units** (Mono-letter method and vintages, projection period 2028-12-31 to 2039-06-15).

Band across scenarios: 179,100 - 228,584 units. The band is driven by the
overcrowding/cost-burden formula (Mono letter's rate-difference vs
Humboldt letter's dilution-to-US-rate), ACS/LODES/PIT vintages, and
the projection-period end date - not by invented uncertainty.

- **4** Projected Households: DOF P-2A/P-4 (b2024_snapshot) interpolated; GQ held at E-5 2025 (DOF does not publish GQ projections)
- **5a** + Vacancy Adjustment, Existing Households: owner 0.72% vs 2%, renter 3.98% vs 7%
- **5b** + Vacancy Adjustment, Projected Households: healthy rates x projected household growth (54405)
- **6** + Overcrowding Adjustment: region 6.69% vs US 3.40%; rate difference x ACS households (Mono-letter formula)

- **9** + Replacement Adjustment Demolitions (0.10%): 10-yr APR avg 89/yr on 1,204,218 occupied; floor 0.1%, cap 5%; applied to projected occupied households
- **11** + Jobs Housing Relationship Adjustment: LODES 2022 JT01 net in-commute -13,837 / 1.5
- **12** + Homelessness Adjustment: PIT 2024 CA-601 10,605 x share 1.0000 x formation 0.3739
